# Economics Specification

## Purpose

This document defines the economic model of the prediction market protocol.

The goal is to create a transparent, sustainable and user-aligned economic system.

## Core Principles

- Transparent fee structure
- Sustainable protocol economics
- Fair market participation
- Clear treasury rules
- No hidden fees
- Risk-aware market design
- Community-aligned governance
- Economic parameters must be documented before deployment

## Fees

The protocol may charge fees on eligible market activity.

Fee parameters should be:

- Clearly displayed to users
- Defined by governance
- Applied consistently
- Documented publicly

No fee should be hidden from users.

## Market Rules

Each market must define:

- Market question
- Market outcome options
- Opening time
- Closing time
- Resolution conditions
- Resolution source
- Settlement rules
- Applicable fees
- Dispute or resolution process

Market rules must be available before users participate.

## Risk Parameters

The protocol should maintain configurable risk parameters including:

- Maximum position limits
- Market liquidity limits
- Exposure limits
- Settlement limits
- Emergency controls
- Market-specific risk parameters

Risk parameters may be adjusted through the documented governance process.

## Treasury

Protocol treasury funds may be used for:

- Protocol development
- Security and audits
- Infrastructure
- Liquidity programs
- Community initiatives
- Grants
- Operational expenses
- Other governance-approved purposes

Treasury transactions should be transparent and publicly auditable.

## Governance

Economic parameters may be governed by the protocol's governance system.

Governance may control:

- Fee parameters
- Treasury usage
- Risk parameters
- Market parameters
- Approved resolution mechanisms
- Protocol upgrades

Governance rules must be transparent and documented.

## Sustainability

The economic model should aim to maintain long-term protocol sustainability.

Protocol revenue should be used responsibly and should not depend on misleading users or unsustainable incentives.

## Changes

Any major change to economic parameters should be:

1. Documented
2. Reviewed
3. Proposed through the appropriate governance process
4. Approved according to governance rules
5. Communicated before activation where practical

## Status

Early development.

Economic parameters are subject to change before production deployment.
